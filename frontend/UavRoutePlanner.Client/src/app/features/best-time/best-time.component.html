Best departure time

Choose the hour with the most suitable wind for a mission you already have

What does this do?

Wind changes hour by hour, and it decides how far the UAV can fly. Pick one of your missions and a time range, and a route is calculated for every hour of that range using the Open-Meteo wind forecast. Compare the hours, open any of them to see the route, then save the one you like as a new mission. The new mission keeps its departure time as a mark, and your original mission stays unchanged.
